More Than Just A Water Park

The Ultimate Family & School One Day Retreat

Mama's Fun Land is specifically crafted to bring families, friends, school excursions, and tuition groups together under one sunny open-air haven. Situated in the calm surroundings of Butlav, near the 66KV GEB Substation, it is the quickest escape from urban rush.

Whether you want to plunge into clean, filtered swimming pools with rhythmic music, challenge friends on the zip-line, steer off-road 4x4 ATVs, shoot targets in paint-ball arenas, or let youngsters jump freely on trampolines—Mama's Fun Land guarantees pure joy.
